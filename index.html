About Mikael

Clean aircraft are safe aircraft.

I'm Mikael Abuye, or Mick, an aircraft cleaning specialist. My job is to make sure every aircraft I work on looks its best and meets the safety and quality standards the aviation industry demands, from the fuselage and wings to every seat, galley and lavatory in the cabin.

I work methodically, follow airside safety procedures to the letter and treat every aircraft as if passengers were boarding in the next hour, because they often are.

Services

Complete care, nose to tail.

Aircraft Exterior Cleaning

Fuselage, wings, empennage and landing gear washed with approved products to remove dirt, exhaust residue and bugs without harming paint or sensors.

Cabin Cleaning

Turnaround and overnight cabin cleans covering seats, tray tables, carpets, galleys, lavatories and overhead bins, ready for the next boarding.

Deep Cleaning

Scheduled deep cleans and disinfection that reach the hidden areas: seat tracks, sidewall panels, air vents and high-touch surfaces.

Detailing

Polishing, brightwork, leather care and finishing touches that restore a showroom look for business jets and VIP interiors.

Safety Procedures

Strict adherence to airside rules, PPE, chemical handling and manufacturer guidelines, so the job is done safely and the aircraft is never put at risk.
